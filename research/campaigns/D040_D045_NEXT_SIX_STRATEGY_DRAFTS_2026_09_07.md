## D040 — NR4 Contraction Breakout V0

**Family:** volatility contraction / expansion.

**Why it exists:** D038 NR7 produced strong descriptive DEV alpha but failed only the frozen count gate. Toby Crabel's NR4 is a separately documented, less restrictive contraction pattern and should naturally occur more often than NR7. This is hypothesis generation, not rescue.

**Frozen draft rule:**
- D-1 range must be strictly smaller than D-2, D-3 and D-4;
- next broker day first executable ASK break above D-1 high or BID break below D-1 low wins;
- stop at opposite D-1 extreme;
- one trade/day, no reversal;
- stop/EOD only, no management filters;
- same six symbols, FundedNext costs and native Trade Path as D038/D039.

**Correlation warning:** high conceptual correlation with D038/D039. A pass here counts as contraction-family evidence, not a third independent alpha family.

**Evidence basis:** Toby Crabel's NR4 / opening-range-breakout work.

## D044 — Turtle Soup 20-Day False-Break Reversal V0

**Family:** price-only mean reversion / failed breakout.

**Why it exists:** independent from the contraction and broad trend families. Classic Turtle Soup, associated with Linda Bradford Raschke, fades a failed break of a prior multi-day extreme rather than following it.

**Methodology blocker before preregistration:** recover one exact historical rule set from a primary/authoritative source. Secondary descriptions agree on the core 20-day false-break concept but differ on minimum age of the prior extreme and exact re-entry trigger. We will not invent those details and call it a replication.

**Draft Guardian adaptation only if exact rules are recovered:**

- executable tick penetration of prior 20-day extreme;
- deterministic failure/re-entry trigger;
- stop beyond excursion extreme;
- one trade/event;
- fixed EOD or source-faithful exit;
- native Trade Path.

**Status:** reserve, methodology verification required before source.

## D045 — Classic D1 20/10 Donchian Trend Benchmark V0

**Family:** medium-term trend following.

**Why it exists:** D036 tested an H1 Donchian implementation; this draft is a deliberately different **classic daily** benchmark, not a retune of D036. The 20-day breakout / 10-day opposite-channel exit shape is the well-known Turtle System 1 entry/exit form and gives us a clean slow-trend sanity check against our CFD transport.

**Draft rule:**
- LONG on executable break above prior completed 20-D1-bar high;
- SHORT on executable break below prior completed 20-D1-bar low;
- exit long on prior completed 10-D1-bar low break; inverse for short;
- no pyramiding in V0;
- no previous-winner skip rule in V0 unless exact-source replication is chosen instead;
- one position/symbol;
- realistic CFD spread/commission;
- report event count and portfolio overlap because cadence is expected to be low.

**Role:** benchmark/diversifier, not expected to be the main challenge-finishing engine.
