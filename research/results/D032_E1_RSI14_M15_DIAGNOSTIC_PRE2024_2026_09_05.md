## Purpose
Record RSI(14) M15 at each already-confirmed D032 Bullish Doji Star H1 signal, without filtering entries, to test whether oversold RSI helps explain entry quality / +24h outcome.

## Data supplied
PRE2024 scanner runs were supplied for BTCUSD, ETHUSD, DOGUSD plus unregistered LNKUSD and XRPUSD. Primary interpretation remains BTC/ETH/DOG.

Clean core sample matches D032-C1: 79 clean +24h events (BTC 33 / ETH 32 / DOG 14).

RSI sampling was the last fully closed M15 candle. Most clean core rows use the expected bar open at signal_end minus 15 minutes. A small number use stale M15 bars across broker/session gaps; those are excluded from the strict freshness descriptive check where noted.

## Standard oversold threshold clue: RSI <30
RSI<30 was the natural standard threshold discussed before opening these results; it was not applied by the scanner.

Core BTC/ETH/DOG:
- n = 10 / 79 clean events;
- mean +24h = +448.2 bps;
- median +24h = +179.8 bps;
- mean +24h = +2.017R/event;
- win rate = 70%;
- mean pre24 MAE = -2.700R;
- median pre24 MAE = -1.367R.

The large mean is highly outlier-sensitive: removing the largest +24h R event reduces mean from +2.017R to about +0.501R; removing the two largest leaves approximately -0.10R. Therefore this is a strong clue, not evidence sufficient to adopt a filter.

Critically, RSI<30 does NOT solve the entry-location problem in PRE2024. Among +24h winners, RSI<30 winner median MAE is about -1.35R versus about -0.82R for RSI>=30 winners. It may select violent capitulation/reversal events with larger eventual payoff, but it does not make the initial entry obviously safer.

## Cross-market exploratory observation
Using only rows with a fresh M15 bar exactly 15 minutes before the H1 signal, the RSI<30 subset is positive in mean on all five supplied CFDs:
- BTC: n3, mean ~+560 bps;
- ETH: n3, mean ~+385 bps;
- DOG: n4, mean ~+412 bps;
- LNK: n5, mean ~+245 bps;
- XRP: n2, mean ~+324 bps.

Pooled five-market fresh-M15 RSI<30: n17, mean ~+374 bps, median ~+319 bps, mean ~+1.81R, win rate ~70.6%. The sample is still tiny and bootstrap uncertainty crosses zero; LNK/XRP remain transport diagnostics, not core evidence.

## Decision
- Do not add RSI to production from PRE2024 diagnostics.
- Do not claim RSI improves stop placement; it did not clearly reduce MAE.
- The standard RSI(14) M15 <30 subset is sufficiently interesting to freeze now for a POST2024 conditional validation run before RSI values in that interval are inspected.
- Any POST2024 validation remains an adaptation/conditional-filter test, not a fresh independent confirmation of the underlying Doji entry because +24h Doji outcomes for 2024-2026 were already seen in D032 discovery.
- Frequency warning: PRE2024 core RSI<30 retained only 10/79 clean events (~13%), so even if it validates it becomes a sparse high-conviction sleeve, not the required active challenge engine.
